Test 1 · Q131–134

Questions 131-134 refer to the following article.

Norlich Agricultural Brands Opens New Frozen Vegetable Plant

Norlich Agricultural Brands has completed construction of its new vegetable-processing plant in Eamley. It replaces an outdated (131) located nearby. The new plant can process 10 million kilos of corn, peas, and beans annually. (132). The new plant will (133) use 25 percent less water per kilo of product.

The vegetables processed at the new site will come from regional growers. Norlich works closely with its local network of suppliers (134) that vegetables are picked and processed when they are ripest.

✅ 正确答案正解 (B) facility
新工厂取代了附近一个“过时的设施”，facility 指工厂等设施，与 plant 对应，B正确。shop“商店”、machine“机器”、monument“纪念碑”都不能与 plant 相对应。新工場は近くにある「時代遅れの施設」に取って代わるもので、facility は工場などの施設を指し plant に対応するので、Bが正解。shop「店」、machine「機械」、monument「記念碑」は plant に対応しない。

前一句给出数据“每年可加工1000万公斤”，A 的 This figure（这一数字）直接承接该数据，并与旧工厂比较，后句又谈新工厂节水，逻辑连贯，A正确。B、C谈公司历史和员工家族，偏离产能话题；D谈植物性饮食，与工厂无关。前の文で「年間1,000万キロを加工できる」というデータが示されている。AのThis figure（この数字）はそのデータを直接受けて旧工場と比較しており、次の文で新工場の節水の話に移るので、論理がつながる。Aが正解。B・Cは会社の歴史や従業員の家族の話で生産能力の話題から外れ、Dは植物性の食生活の話で工場と無関係。

句子已有谓语 works，空格引导目的状语“以确保蔬菜在最成熟时采摘加工”，不定式 to ensure 正确。ensured/ensures/will ensure 都是谓语形式，句中不能再出现第二个谓语（没有连词）。文にはすでに述語 works があり、空所は「野菜が最も熟した時期に収穫・加工されるようにするために」という目的を表す部分を導く。不定詞 to ensure が正解。ensured/ensures/will ensure はいずれも述語の形で、接続詞なしに2つ目の述語は置けない。

Test 1 · Q139
👆 点选项作答，或点这里看答案👆 選択肢をタップ、またはここで答えを見る
✅ 正确答案正解 (A) to work
the opportunity to do“做…的机会”，不定式作后置定语修饰 opportunity，A正确。works 是谓语形式；that worked 时态和语义不对（机会尚未实现）；opportunity 后不直接接 -ing（需 of doing）。the opportunity to do「～する機会」で、不定詞が後ろから opportunity を修飾するので、Aが正解。works は述語の形、that worked は時制も意味も合わない（機会はまだ実現していない）。opportunity の後に -ing を直接続けることはできない（of doing が必要）。

Director Lee Jiang Discusses *Anchored Ascent*

GALWAY (15 October)—After a month's delay, *Anchored Ascent* premiered at the Pinelands Film Festival this week. This film tells the true story of acclaimed mountain climber Quinn Deacon.

According to the director, Lee Jiang, the film crew endured several production setbacks that caused concern among investors. First, lengthy negotiations with actor Vernon Scafidi ultimately fell through when his salary demands could not be met. "Luckily, Mark Tobel was available to take on the role, and he joined our cast right at the beginning of February," Mr. Jiang explained, "so we still could have met our production deadlines. However, filming of outdoor scenes was postponed for six weeks due to an unusually heavy snow season and impassable roads."

The delay in outdoor camerawork meant Long Road Productions had to pay for an extension to their filming permit. "In the end, though, starting a month later meant longer daylight hours, helping us make up some of the lost time." If the audience response at the Pinelands Film Festival is any indication, the movie's financial backers will be quite pleased with their return on investment.

*Anchored Ascent* is now set for general distribution to cinemas on 1 November.
